Why lemurs matter

Lemurs are found nowhere else on Earth except Madagascar and its neighboring islands. They have evolved in isolation for over 60 million years, creating an extraordinary diversity of over 100 species. As seed dispersers and pollinators, lemurs are critical to maintaining Madagascar's unique ecosystems.

Madagascar has lost approximately 90% of its original forest cover due to slash-and-burn agriculture (known as tavy), logging, mining, and charcoal production. Climate change is further stressing habitats. Without intervention, many lemur species will be extinct within our lifetimes.
